Spot Bad Arguments Like a Pro

Why This Matters: Fallacies are used daily to manipulate you. Learn to spot them!

Top 5 Fallacies You Must Know:

  • Ad Hominem: Attack the person, not the argument
  • Straw Man: Misrepresent the opponent's view
  • False Dilemma: "Either this or that" when more options exist
  • Appeal to Popularity: "Everyone believes it!"
  • Slippery Slope: "Small step leads to disaster"

💡 Ethiopian Example:

A politician says: "Vote for me because I'm from your region!" This is Appeal to Emotion + Appeal to Tradition.

📌 Quick Tip:

Fallacies look convincing but are logically flawed. Spot them to protect yourself!
