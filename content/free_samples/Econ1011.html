The Heart of Economics

Why This Matters: Every time you buy or sell anything, you're participating in economics.

Law of Demand

When price INCREASES, quantity demanded DECREASES.

Example: When coffee prices rise in Addis, people buy less coffee.

Law of Supply

When price INCREASES, quantity supplied INCREASES.

Example: When teff prices rise, farmers grow more teff.

Profit Maximization

Profit = Total Revenue - Total Cost

Firms maximize profit when MR = MC.

📌 Key Points:

  • Demand: Price ↑ → Qty ↓
  • Supply: Price ↑ → Qty ↑
  • Profit Max: MR = MC
