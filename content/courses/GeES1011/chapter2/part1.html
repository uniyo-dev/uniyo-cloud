2.1 Introduction to Geology

Geology is the scientific study of the Earth's origin, structure, composition, and the processes that shape it over time. For geographers, geology is essential because it explains landforms, rocks, minerals, and natural resources.

Key Definition: Geology is the science that deals with the Earth's physical structure, substance, history, and the processes acting on it.

Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa have a COMPLEX geological history spanning BILLIONS of years. The region's geology explains its mountains, plateaus, rift valleys, and volcanic features.

2.2 The Geologic Processes: Endogenic and Exogenic Forces

Geologic processes that shape the Earth's surface are divided into TWO major categories: Endogenic (Internal) and Exogenic (External) forces.

Endogenic Forces (Internal)

Endogenic Forces: Forces originating from INSIDE the Earth that build up the Earth's surface. They create mountains, plateaus, rift valleys, and volcanoes.
Endogenic Process Description Example in Ethiopia
Folding Bending of rock layers due to compression Mountain ranges
Faulting Breaking and displacement of rocks Great Rift Valley
Volcanism Eruption of magma to surface Erta Ale volcano, Dallol
Uplift Rising of large land areas Ethiopian Highlands
Earthquakes Sudden movement along faults Rift Valley quakes

Exogenic Forces (External)

Exogenic Forces: Forces originating from OUTSIDE (atmosphere, water, ice, wind) that WEAR DOWN the Earth's surface. They create valleys, canyons, and plains through erosion.
Exogenic Process Agent Example
Weathering Temperature, water, chemicals Rock disintegration
Erosion by Water Rivers, rain Blue Nile Gorge
Erosion by Wind Wind Desert rock formations
Erosion by Ice Glaciers U-shaped valleys
Mass Wasting Gravity Landslides

2.3 The Geological Time Scale and Age Dating Techniques

The Earth is approximately 4.6 BILLION years old. Geologists have divided this vast time into EONS, ERAS, PERIODS, and EPOCHS.

Age Dating Techniques

Relative Dating

Relative Dating: Determines the AGE of rocks/events in relation to OTHER rocks/events. It does NOT give an exact age, only which is OLDER/YOUNGER.
Relative Dating Principle Description
Superposition Older rocks are at bottom, younger on top
Cross-cutting Relationships Faults/intrusions are younger than rocks they cut
Fossil Correlation Rocks with same fossils are same age

Absolute Dating

Absolute Dating: Determines the EXACT age of rocks in YEARS using radioactive decay of elements.
Absolute Dating Method How It Works
Radiometric Dating Measures decay of radioactive isotopes (Carbon-14, Uranium-238)
Carbon-14 Dating For organic materials up to ~50,000 years old
Potassium-Argon Dating For volcanic rocks millions of years old

📌 Key Points to Remember:

  • Geology = study of Earth's structure and history
  • Endogenic = Internal forces (BUILD surface)
  • Exogenic = External forces (WEAR DOWN surface)
  • Earth is ~4.6 billion years old
  • Four Eras: Precambrian, Paleozoic, Mesozoic, Cenozoic
  • Relative Dating = order only | Absolute Dating = exact age
  • Ethiopian Rift Valley = result of ENDOGENIC faulting
