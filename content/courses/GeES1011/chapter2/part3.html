2.5 Rock and Mineral Resources of Ethiopia

Ethiopia is endowed with SIGNIFICANT mineral resources distributed across various geological formations. While the country's mineral sector is still developing, several valuable minerals have been identified and some are being exploited.

Key Definition: Mineral resources are naturally occurring substances (metals, non-metals, gemstones) found in rocks that have economic value and can be extracted for use.

2.5.1 Brief Facts and Current State of Main Minerals in Ethiopia

Gold

Gold in Ethiopia:
• Ethiopia's MOST IMPORTANT mineral
• Found in: Western (Wollega), Southern (Adola, Lega Dembi), Northern (Tigray)
• Lega Dembi Mine = largest gold mine in Ethiopia
• Used for: Jewelry, electronics, investment

Platinum

Platinum in Ethiopia:
• Ethiopia is one of the FEW countries with platinum deposits in Africa
• Found in: Western Ethiopia (Yubdo, Birbir River)
• Used for: Catalytic converters, jewelry, industrial applications

Tantalum

Tantalum in Ethiopia:
• High-value mineral used in ELECTRONICS
• Found in: Southern Ethiopia (Kenticha, Adola)
• Used for: Mobile phones, capacitors, medical equipment

2.5.2 Mineral Potential Sites of Ethiopia

The Western and South-Western Greenstone Belt

Western Greenstone Belt:
• MOST IMPORTANT mineral zone in Ethiopia
• Contains: GOLD, PLATINUM, base metals
• Locations: Wollega, Benishangul-Gumuz, Gambella
• Key mines: Lega Dembi (gold), Yubdo (platinum)

The Southern Greenstone Belt

Southern Greenstone Belt:
• Second most important mineral zone
• Contains: GOLD, TANTALUM, NICKEL
• Locations: Adola, Kenticha, Moyale
• Key features: Ultramafic rocks, gold-quartz veins

The Northern Greenstone Belt (Tigray)

Northern Greenstone Belt (Tigray):
• Contains: GOLD, BASE METALS, GEMSTONES
• Locations: Shire, Aksum, Adwa areas
• Key features: Volcanogenic massive sulfide deposits

📌 Key Points to Remember:

  • Gold = Ethiopia's MOST IMPORTANT mineral
  • Platinum = rare, found in Western Ethiopia (Yubdo)
  • Tantalum = used in electronics (Kenticha)
  • Potash = found in Dallol (Afar) for fertilizer
  • Three Greenstone Belts: Western (best), Southern, Northern
  • Western Belt = Gold + Platinum
  • Lega Dembi = largest gold mine in Ethiopia
