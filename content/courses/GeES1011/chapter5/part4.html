5.5 Climate Change/Global Warming: Causes, Consequences and Response Mechanisms

Climate change refers to LONG-TERM shifts in global or regional climate patterns — primarily caused by human activities that release greenhouse gases.

Key Definition: Climate change is a significant and lasting change in temperature, precipitation, and weather patterns over decades or longer, primarily caused by anthropogenic (human) activities increasing greenhouse gas concentrations.

5.5.1 Current Trends of Climate in Ethiopia

Trends in Temperature Variability

Temperature Trends in Ethiopia:
• Average temperature has INCREASED by ~1°C since 1960
• Rate of warming: ~0.28°C per decade
• MORE hot days and FEWER cold nights
• Warming is FASTER in lowlands than highlands
• More frequent heat waves
• Projected: Temperature could rise 2-4°C by 2100

Trends in Rainfall Variability

Rainfall Trends in Ethiopia:
• HIGH variability (unpredictable rainfall)
• Some areas: DECREASING annual rainfall
• Others: More INTENSE rainfall events (flooding)
• SHIFTING of seasons (Kiremt starting later)
• More frequent DROUGHTS and FLOODS
• Belg season becoming less reliable

5.5.2 Causes of Climate Change

Natural Causes

Natural Causes:
1. Volcanic Eruptions — Release ash and sulfur dioxide (temporary cooling)
2. Solar Variations — Changes in sun's energy output
3. Earth's Orbital Changes (Milankovitch Cycles) — Changes in orbit, tilt, wobble
4. Ocean Currents — El Niño/La Niña phenomena
5. Natural Forest Fires — Release CO₂

Anthropogenic (Human) Causes

Anthropogenic Causes:
1. Burning of FOSSIL FUELS — Coal, oil, natural gas → CO₂
2. DEFORESTATION — Cutting forests reduces carbon absorption
3. AGRICULTURE — Methane from livestock, rice paddies
4. INDUSTRIAL PROCESSES — Cement production, manufacturing
5. WASTE DECOMPOSITION — Landfills release methane
6. LAND USE CHANGE — Urbanization, agriculture expansion

5.5.3 Consequences of Climate Change

Impacts on Human Health

Health Impacts:
• HEAT STRESS and heat-related deaths
• Spread of MALARIA to new (higher altitude) areas
• WATER-BORNE diseases (cholera, typhoid)
• MALNUTRITION from crop failure
• RESPIRATORY diseases from air pollution

Impacts on Water Resources

Water Impacts:
• REDUCED river flows (drying rivers)
• Shrinking LAKES and WETLANDS
• Decreased GROUNDWATER recharge
• More frequent FLOODS and DROUGHTS
• Water SCARCITY for irrigation and drinking

Impact on Agriculture

Agricultural Impacts:
• REDUCED crop yields (heat stress)
• UNRELIABLE rainfall (droughts)
• SHIFTING of crop zones (coffee moving higher)
• INCREASED pest and disease outbreaks
• Livestock deaths from drought
• FOOD INSECURITY and famine risk

Impact on Ecosystem

Ecosystem Impacts:
• LOSS of BIODIVERSITY
• Extinction of ENDEMIC species
• FOREST DIEBACK
• Coral reef BLEACHING (in Red Sea)
• SHIFTING of vegetation zones

5.5.4 Climate Response Mechanisms

Mitigation

MITIGATION (Reducing greenhouse gases):
Strategies:
1. REFORESTATION and AFFORESTATION (plant trees)
2. RENEWABLE ENERGY (solar, wind, hydro, geothermal)
3. ENERGY EFFICIENCY (reduce consumption)
4. ELECTRIC VEHICLES (reduce fossil fuels)
5. CARBON SEQUESTRATION (capture and store CO₂)
6. SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE (reduce methane)
7. WASTE MANAGEMENT (reduce, reuse, recycle)

Adaptation

ADAPTATION (Adjusting to impacts):
Strategies:
1. DROUGHT-RESISTANT crops
2. WATER HARVESTING and conservation
3. IRRIGATION development
4. EARLY WARNING SYSTEMS
5. CLIMATE-SMART agriculture
6. DIVERSIFYING livelihoods
7. BUILDING resilient infrastructure
8. COMMUNITY-based adaptation
Feature Mitigation Adaptation
Focus CAUSE of climate change EFFECTS of climate change
Goal Reduce GHG emissions Cope with impacts
Examples Reforestation, solar energy Drought-resistant crops
Time Scale Long-term Immediate to medium-term

📌 Key Points to Remember:

  • Climate change = long-term shift in climate patterns
  • Ethiopia warming ~0.28°C per decade
  • Natural causes: Volcanoes, solar, orbital changes
  • Anthropogenic causes: Fossil fuels, deforestation, agriculture
  • Consequences: Health, water, agriculture, ecosystem impacts
  • MITIGATION = Reducing GHG emissions
  • ADAPTATION = Coping with impacts
