5.1 Introduction to Climate

Climate is the LONG-TERM average of weather conditions (temperature, rainfall, humidity, wind) in a specific area over a period of at least 30 years. It differs from WEATHER, which is the SHORT-TERM state of the atmosphere.

Key Distinction:
• Weather: Short-term atmospheric conditions (hourly, daily)
• Climate: Long-term average of weather (30+ years)
• Ethiopia's climate: Determined by LATITUDE, ALTITUDE, and MONSOON WINDS

Ethiopia's climate is UNIQUE — despite its tropical location (near the equator), its HIGH ALTITUDE creates moderate temperatures and diverse climate zones, ranging from hot deserts to alpine conditions.

5.2 Elements and Controls of Weather and Climate

5.2.1 Controls of Weather and Climate

Several factors CONTROL (determine) Ethiopia's weather and climate:

Latitude

Latitude's Effect:
• Ethiopia lies between 3°N and 15°N latitude
• TROPICAL location → receives DIRECT sunlight year-round
• Nearer to EQUATOR = WARMER temperatures
• Determines the amount of SOLAR RADIATION received
• Ethiopia's tropical position gives it WARM temperatures throughout the year

Inclination of the Earth's Axis

Earth's Tilt:
• Earth's axis is tilted at 23.5° from vertical
• This tilt causes SEASONS
• When Northern Hemisphere tilts TOWARD sun = SUMMER (northern)
• When Northern Hemisphere tilts AWAY from sun = WINTER (northern)
• Ethiopia (in Northern Hemisphere) experiences this seasonal variation

Equinoxes and Solstices

Event Date What Happens Significance for Ethiopia
Vernal (Spring) Equinox March 21 Equal day and night Sun overhead at equator
Autumn Equinox September 23 Equal day and night Sun overhead at equator
Summer Solstice June 21 Longest day (N. Hemisphere) Sun overhead at Tropic of Cancer
Winter Solstice December 21 Shortest day (N. Hemisphere) Sun overhead at Tropic of Capricorn

Altitude

Altitude's Effect on Climate:
• Ethiopia's elevation ranges from -125m (Dallol) to 4,550m (Ras Dashen)
• HIGHER altitude = COOLER temperatures
• Temperature decreases by approximately 6.5°C per 1,000m (normal lapse rate)
• Ethiopia's HIGHLANDS enjoy moderate climate DESPITE tropical location
• Addis Ababa (2,400m) has average temperature of 16°C

Types of Lapse Rate

The Lapse Rate is the rate at which temperature changes with altitude.

Lapse Rate Type Rate Description
Environmental (Atmospheric) Lapse Rate ~6.5°C per 1,000m Actual temperature change with altitude in the atmosphere
Dry Adiabatic Lapse Rate ~10°C per 1,000m Cooling of RISING DRY air parcel
Wet (Saturated) Adiabatic Lapse Rate ~5°C per 1,000m Cooling of RISING SATURATED (cloudy) air

📌 Key Points to Remember:

  • Weather = short-term | Climate = long-term (30+ years)
  • Ethiopia: 3°N-15°N (tropical)
  • Earth's axis tilted at 23.5°
  • Equinoxes: Equal day/night (March 21, Sept 23)
  • Solstices: Longest/shortest day (June 21, Dec 21)
  • Altitude: Higher = Cooler
  • Three lapse rates: Environmental, Dry, Wet Adiabatic
