3.1 Introduction to Topography

Topography refers to the physical features and shape of the Earth's surface — mountains, valleys, plateaus, plains, and their arrangement. Ethiopia has one of the most DIVERSE topographies in Africa, with elevations ranging from 125 meters BELOW sea level (Dallol) to 4,550 meters ABOVE sea level (Ras Dashen).

Key Definition: Topography is the study of the physical features of the Earth's surface including their elevation, shape, and distribution.

3.2 The Physiographic Divisions of Ethiopia

Ethiopia can be divided into FOUR major physiographic regions:

Physiographic Division Elevation Key Features
1. Western Highlands 2000-3000m Plateaus, mountains, deep gorges
2. Western Lowlands 500-1500m Plains, river valleys
3. Eastern Highlands 2000-3000m Plateaus, mountains
4. Eastern Lowlands Below 500m Deserts, semi-arid plains

3.2.1 The Western Highlands and Lowlands

3.2.1.1 The Western Highlands

The WESTERN HIGHLANDS are the LARGEST and most important physiographic region in Ethiopia. They include several sub-regions:

The Tigray Plateau

Tigray Plateau:
• Located in NORTHERN Ethiopia
• Elevation: 2000-3000 meters
• Composed of SANDSTONE and LIMESTONE
• Deeply dissected by rivers (Tekeze, Mereb)
• Contains historic sites (Aksum, Adwa)

The North Central Massifs

North Central Massifs:
• Located in Amhara region (Gondar, Wollo)
• HIGHEST mountains in Ethiopia
• Ras Dashen (4,550m) = highest peak
• Contains Simien Mountains National Park
• Composed of volcanic basalt

The Shewa Plateau / Central Highland

Shewa Plateau:
• Located in CENTRAL Ethiopia (Addis Ababa area)
• Elevation: 2500-3000 meters
• Addis Ababa (capital) located here
• Drained by Awash River
• Volcanic features (Zuqualla, Entoto)

The South Western Highlands

South Western Highlands:
• Located in Oromia, SNNPR (Jimma, Illubabor)
• HIGHEST RAINFALL in Ethiopia
• Dense forests (coffee growing regions)
• Drained by Omo and Ghibe rivers
• Lower elevation than northern highlands

3.2.1.2 Western Lowlands

The WESTERN LOWLANDS are the low-lying areas along Ethiopia's western border with Sudan and South Sudan. They include several river lowlands:

Lowland Location Key River Elevation
Tekeze Lowland NW Ethiopia Tekeze River 500-1500m
Abay-Dinder Lowland W Ethiopia Abay (Blue Nile) 500-1500m
Baro Lowland SW Ethiopia Baro River 500-1000m
Ghibe Lowland SW Ethiopia Ghibe/Omo River 500-1500m

📌 Key Points to Remember:

  • Ethiopia's elevation: -125m (Dallol) to 4,550m (Ras Dashen)
  • Four physiographic regions: W Highlands, W Lowlands, E Highlands, E Lowlands
  • Western Highlands = LARGEST region
  • Tigray Plateau = sandstone/limestone, historic sites
  • North Central Massifs = highest (Ras Dashen, Simien Mountains)
  • Shewa Plateau = Addis Ababa located here
  • SW Highlands = highest rainfall, coffee growing
  • Western Lowlands = river valleys (Tekeze, Abay, Baro, Ghibe)
