3.2.2 The Southeastern Highlands and Lowlands

The SOUTHEASTERN region of Ethiopia comprises the EASTERN HIGHLANDS (part of the eastern plateau) and the SOUTHEASTERN LOWLANDS. This region extends from the Rift Valley eastward toward the Somali border.

Key Definition: The Southeastern Highlands are the elevated areas east of the Rift Valley, while the Southeastern Lowlands are the low-lying plains extending toward Somalia.

3.2.2.1 The Southeastern Highlands

The SOUTHEASTERN HIGHLANDS include several distinct highland sub-regions:

Arsi Highland

Arsi Highland:
• Located in OROMIA region (east of Rift Valley)
• Elevation: 2000-4000 meters
• Composed of volcanic basalt
• Known for WHEAT and BARLEY production
• Contains Mount Chilalo (4,036m)
• Drained by Wabishebelle and Awash rivers

Bale Highlands (Bale Massifs)

Bale Highlands:
• Located in OROMIA region (southeast of Arsi)
• SECOND HIGHEST mountain range in Ethiopia
• Mount Batu (4,307m) = second highest peak
• Contains BALE MOUNTAINS NATIONAL PARK
• Home to Ethiopian wolf (rare endemic species)
• Source of Wabishebelle River

Sidama Highlands

Sidama Highlands:
• Located in SIDAMA region (south of Rift Valley)
• Elevation: 2000-3000 meters
• Dense population
• Known for COFFEE production (arabica)
• Fertile volcanic soils

Hararge Plateau

Hararge Plateau:
• Located in EASTERN OROMIA and HARARI region
• Elevation: 1500-3000 meters
• Composed of SANDSTONE and LIMESTONE
• Historic city of HARAR located here
• Known for CHAT (khat) production
• Drained by Wabishebelle and Ghenalle rivers

3.2.2.2 Southeastern Lowlands

The SOUTHEASTERN LOWLANDS are the low-lying plains that extend from the highlands toward the Somali border. They are SEMI-ARID and sparsely populated.

Ghenalle Plain

Ghenalle Plain:
• Located in SOUTHEAST Ethiopia (Somali region)
• Drained by GHENALLE RIVER
• Semi-arid climate with low rainfall
• Elevation: 500-1500 meters
• Used for pastoralism (livestock herding)

Wabishebelle Plain

Wabishebelle Plain:
• Located in EAST-CENTRAL Ethiopia (Somali region)
• Drained by WABISHEBELLE RIVER (longest river entirely in Ethiopia)
• Semi-arid to arid climate
• Elevation: 500-1500 meters
• Important for irrigation agriculture

📌 Key Points to Remember:

  • Southeastern Highlands are EAST of Rift Valley
  • Arsi = Wheat/Barley, Mount Chilalo
  • Bale = Mount Batu (2nd highest), Bale National Park
  • Sidama = Coffee production
  • Hararge = Harar city, Chat production
  • Ghenalle Plain = Drained by Ghenalle River
  • Wabishebelle Plain = Drained by Wabishebelle River
  • Both lowlands = semi-arid, pastoralism
