1.1 Geography: Definition, Scope and Themes

1.1.1 Meaning of Geography

The word Geography comes from two Greek words:

  • "Geo" = Earth
  • "Graphia" = Description/To Write
Key Definition: Geography is the scientific study of the Earth's surface, including the distribution and interaction of physical features, climate, and human activities across space and time.

Geography is a BRIDGE discipline connecting the NATURAL sciences (Physical Geography) and the SOCIAL sciences (Human Geography).

1.1.2 The Scope, Approaches and Themes of Geography

Five Themes of Geography:

Theme Key Question Example
1. Location Where is it? Ethiopia is at 8°N, 38°E
2. Place What is it like? Highlands, diverse cultures
3. Human-Environment Interaction How do people relate to environment? Farming on terraced hillsides
4. Movement How do people/goods move? Migration, trade routes
5. Region How are areas similar? Highland vs Lowland regions

1.2 Location, Shape and Size of Ethiopia and the Horn

1.2.1 Location of Ethiopia

Absolute Location: Ethiopia is located between 3°N - 15°N latitude and 33°E - 48°E longitude.

Ethiopia is located in the HORN OF AFRICA — the easternmost projection of the African continent.

Implications of Ethiopia's Location:

Implication Effect
Climate Tropical location → warm temperatures, seasonal rainfall
Socio-Cultural Crossroads of Africa & Middle East → diverse cultures, religions
Political Near Red Sea → strategic importance, trade routes

1.2.2 Size of Ethiopia

Total Area: Approximately 1,100,000 km² (about 1.1 million square kilometers)
Ethiopia is the 10th largest country in Africa.

📌 Key Points to Remember:

  • Geography = Geo (Earth) + Graphia (Description)
  • Five Themes: Location, Place, Human-Environment, Movement, Region
  • Ethiopia: 3°N-15°N, 33°E-48°E
  • Area: ~1.1 million km²
  • Location in Horn of Africa
