3.1. What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)

🔑 Activity 3.1 (From Module):
  • How do you define the word Artificial? And the word Intelligence?

Breaking Down the Words:

  • Artificial: Defines "man-made." It means something that is not natural but created by humans.
  • Intelligence: Defines "thinking power," or "the ability to learn and solve problems." It is the capacity to acquire and apply knowledge.

Therefore, Artificial Intelligence means "a man-made thinking power."

Formal Definition of AI:

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science by which we can create intelligent machines which can behave like a human, think like humans, and are able to make decisions.

Understanding Intelligence:

Intelligence, as we know, is the ability to acquire and apply knowledge. Knowledge is the information acquired through experience. Experience is the knowledge gained through exposure (training).

💡 Key Insight:

Summing the terms up, we get artificial intelligence as the "copy of something natural (i.e., human beings 'WHO' is capable of acquiring and applying the information it has gained through exposure)."

3.1.1. Need for Artificial Intelligence

🔑 Activity 3.3 (From Module):
  • Why we need AI at this time?

The module outlines two primary reasons why we need AI today:

  1. To create expert systems that exhibit intelligent behavior with the capability to learn, demonstrate, explain, and advise its users.
  2. Helping machines find solutions to complex problems like humans do, and applying them as algorithms in a computer-friendly manner.

💡 Real World Example (Ethiopia - Expert Systems):

In Ethiopia, AI-powered expert systems are being developed to advise farmers on crop diseases. For example, a farmer can take a photo of a sick plant leaf using a mobile app. The AI system learns from thousands of previous images, demonstrates the diagnosis on the screen, explains the cause, and advises the farmer on which pesticide to use. This is a perfect example of why we need AI—to bring expert-level knowledge to remote areas.

The module lists the following main goals of Artificial Intelligence:

3.1.3. What Comprises to Artificial Intelligence?

🔑 Activity 3.5 (From Module):
  • To make a machine learn and make a decision like humans do, AI requires the knowledge of some disciplines. Write down some disciplines which AI requires?

AI is Multidisciplinary:

Artificial Intelligence is not just a part of computer science; even though it is vast, it requires lots of other factors that can contribute to it. To create the AI-first, we should know how intelligence is composed. Intelligence is an intangible part of our brain which is a combination of Reasoning, learning, problem-solving, perception, language understanding, etc.

To achieve the above factors for a machine or software, Artificial Intelligence requires the knowledge of the following disciplines:

  • Mathematics: For algorithms, logic, and optimization.
  • Biology: For understanding neural networks and biological inspiration.
  • Psychology: For understanding human thought processes and cognitive behavior.
  • Sociology: For understanding social interactions and human behavior.
  • Computer Science: For programming, data structures, and system design.
  • Neurons Study: For understanding how the brain processes information (Neural Networks).
  • Statistics: For probability, data analysis, and machine learning models.

3.1.4. Advantages of Artificial Intelligence

🔑 Activity 3.6 (From Module):
  • What do we get from using AI technology instead of previous reactive technology?
AdvantageDescriptionExample
High Accuracy AI machines are prone to fewer errors and have high accuracy as they take decisions based on pre-experience or information. AI-powered medical imaging can detect tumors with greater accuracy than human doctors.
High-Speed AI systems are very high-speed and make fast decisions. AI systems can beat a chess champion in the game of chess. Google's DeepMind AI can analyze millions of moves in seconds.
High Reliability AI machines are highly reliable and can perform the same action multiple times with high accuracy without getting tired. Factory robots performing the same welding task 10,000 times with perfect precision.
Useful for Risky Areas AI machines can be helpful in situations such as defusing a bomb, exploring the ocean floor, or entering a volcano, where employing a human can be risky. Boston Dynamics robots exploring disaster zones after earthquakes.
Digital Assistant AI can be very useful to provide digital assistants to users. AI technology is currently used by various E-commerce websites to show products as per customer requirements. Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant, and TeleBirr's AI-driven customer support.
Useful as a Public Utility AI can be very useful for public utilities such as self-driving cars, facial recognition for security, natural language processing (for search engines, spell checkers, translation), etc. Google Translate, Face ID on smartphones, and self-driving taxis.

3.1.5. Disadvantages of Artificial Intelligence

🔑 Activity 3.7 (From Module):
  • As we all know, engineering is a tradeoff; improving or enhancing in one aspect will lead you to worsen or deteriorating in another aspect. Write down some disadvantages of AI?
DisadvantageDescriptionExample
High Cost The hardware and software requirement of AI is very costly as it requires lots of maintenance to meet current world requirements. Building an AI supercomputer costs hundreds of millions of dollars.
Can't think out of the box Even though we are making smarter machines with AI, they still cannot work out of the box. The robot will only do that work for which they are trained or programmed. An AI that plays chess cannot automatically learn to play soccer.
No feelings and emotions AI machines can be outstanding performers, but they do not have feelings. They cannot make any kind of emotional attachment with humans and may sometimes be harmful if proper care is not taken. An AI cannot comfort a grieving friend; it lacks empathy.
Increase dependence on machines With the increment of technology, people are getting more dependent on devices and hence they are losing their mental capabilities. People rely on GPS and Google Maps instead of learning how to read physical maps.
No Original Creativity As humans are so creative and can imagine some new ideas, AI machines cannot beat this power of human intelligence and cannot be creative and imaginative. AI can generate music, but it still lacks the deep emotional storytelling of human composers.
