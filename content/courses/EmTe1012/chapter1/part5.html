1.5.1 Emerging technology trends in 2019

The module lists the following as the key emerging technology trends that were gaining momentum in 2019. These trends represent the cutting edge of innovation at that time, and most have only grown more influential since.

Technology TrendBrief DescriptionReal-World Impact
5G Networks The fifth generation of mobile network technology, offering ultra-fast speeds, low latency, and the ability to connect billions of devices. Enables autonomous vehicles, smart cities, and seamless streaming. In Ethiopia, 5G trials are underway to support the growing digital economy.
Artificial Intelligence (AI) The simulation of human intelligence in machines, including learning, reasoning, and self-correction. Powers everything from voice assistants (Siri, Alexa) to fraud detection in banking.
Autonomous Devices Devices that can operate independently without human intervention, using sensors and AI to navigate and make decisions. Examples include self-driving cars, delivery drones, and autonomous agricultural robots.
Blockchain A decentralized, distributed ledger technology that records transactions across multiple computers securely and transparently. Used for cryptocurrencies (Bitcoin), supply chain tracking, and secure voting systems.
Augmented Analytics The use of AI and machine learning to automate data preparation, insight generation, and data storytelling. Helps businesses make data-driven decisions without needing a team of data scientists.
Digital Twins A digital replica of a physical object, process, or system that can be used for simulation, analysis, and prediction. Used in manufacturing to predict machine failures before they happen, and in urban planning to simulate traffic flow.
Enhanced Edge Computing Processing data closer to the source (at the "edge" of the network) rather than in a centralized cloud server, reducing latency. Critical for real-time applications like autonomous driving and industrial IoT.
Immersive Experiences in Smart Spaces Combining Augmented Reality (AR), Virtual Reality (VR), and smart sensors to create interactive, immersive environments. Used in smart retail (virtual try-ons), education (virtual classrooms), and entertainment (VR gaming).

1.5.2 Some emerging technologies that will shape the future of you and your business

🚨 CRITICAL MYTH-BUSTER (From Module):

"The future is now or so they say. So-called emerging technologies are taking over our minds more and more each day. These are very high-level emerging technologies though. They sound like tools that will only affect the top tier of technology companies who employ the world's top 1% of geniuses. This is totally wrong."

Why is this myth wrong? The module explicitly debunks the idea that emerging technologies are only for tech giants like Google, Apple, or Microsoft. The reality is that Chatbots, virtual/augmented reality, blockchain, Ephemeral Apps, and Artificial Intelligence are already shaping your life whether you like it or not.

💡 Real World Example (Ethiopia - How these affect daily life):

  • Chatbots: Many Ethiopian banks (like Dashen Bank and Commercial Bank of Ethiopia) now use chatbots on their websites and Telegram channels to answer customer inquiries 24/7, reducing the need to wait in long queues.
  • VR/AR/MR: Ethiopian universities like Addis Ababa University are beginning to use VR for medical training, allowing students to practice surgeries in a virtual environment before touching a real patient.
  • Blockchain: The Ethiopian government has partnered with global blockchain companies to digitize land titles and reduce land disputes using blockchain's transparent and tamper-proof ledger.
  • Ephemeral Apps: Apps like Telegram (widely used in Ethiopia) support ephemeral features like "Secret Chats" and self-destructing messages, which are designed for privacy and temporary use.
  • AI: AI is being used in Ethiopian agriculture to analyze satellite imagery and predict crop diseases, helping farmers in regions like Oromia and Amhara to take preventive action.

The Six Core Technologies Shaping Your Future:

The module specifically highlights these six technologies as the ones that are already having a profound impact on our lives:

TechnologyHow it is Shaping Your LifeEthiopian Context
Chatbots Automated conversation agents that handle customer service, provide information, and assist with tasks 24/7. Ethiopian telecom providers and banks are using chatbots to handle thousands of customer queries daily, reducing wait times.
Virtual, Augmented & Mixed Reality (VR/AR/MR) Immersive technologies that blend the digital and physical worlds. Used for gaming, education, training, and design. Ethiopian construction companies are using AR to visualize building plans on-site; universities are using VR for medical and engineering training.
Blockchain A decentralized digital ledger that ensures transparency, security, and immutability of records. Ethiopia's Ministry of Agriculture is piloting blockchain to track coffee supply chains, ensuring fair payments to farmers and proving the authenticity of Ethiopian coffee to global buyers.
Ephemeral Apps Applications that are designed for temporary or short-term use. They disappear after a certain time or after a specific task is completed. Examples in Ethiopia include temporary event apps for music festivals (like the Ethiopian Jazz Festival) where you can check schedules and buy tickets, and the app disappears after the event.
Artificial Intelligence (AI) The broad field of creating machines that can think, learn, and make decisions like humans. AI is powering TeleBirr's fraud detection algorithms, analyzing millions of transactions to identify suspicious activity in real-time.
