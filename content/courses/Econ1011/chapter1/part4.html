1.7 Decision Making Units and Circular Flow Model

In any economy, there are three main decision-making units:

Unit Role Examples
Households Own factors of production, consume goods Families, individuals
Firms Produce goods and services Factories, shops, farms
Government Regulates, taxes, provides public goods Federal, regional, local

The Circular Flow Model

The Circular Flow Model shows how money, goods, and services flow between households and firms in an economy.

Two Flows in the Model:

Flow Type Direction What Flows
Real Flow Households → Firms Labor, Land, Capital
Real Flow Firms → Households Goods and Services
Money Flow Firms → Households Wages, Rent, Interest, Profit
Money Flow Households → Firms Payment for goods

📌 Key Points:

  • Households OWN factors of production
  • Firms PRODUCE goods and services
  • Real flow = Physical goods and resources
  • Money flow = Payments in opposite direction
  • Both flows move simultaneously in opposite directions
