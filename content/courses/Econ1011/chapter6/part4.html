6.7 Macroeconomic Policy Instruments

Governments use TWO main policy instruments to manage the economy: Monetary Policy and Fiscal Policy.

6.7.1 Monetary Policy

Monetary Policy: Actions by the CENTRAL BANK to control the money supply and interest rates to achieve macroeconomic goals.
Tool How It Works Effect
Open Market Operations Central bank buys/sells government bonds Changes money supply
Reserve Requirements Minimum reserves banks must hold Affects bank lending
Interest Rates Central bank sets benchmark rate Affects borrowing costs

💡 Ethiopia Example:

The National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE) sets interest rates and controls money supply. If inflation is high, NBE raises interest rates to reduce borrowing and spending.

6.7.2 Fiscal Policy

Fiscal Policy: Government decisions about TAXATION and SPENDING to influence the economy.
Type Action When Used
Expansionary Increase spending OR decrease taxes During recession (to stimulate economy)
Contractionary Decrease spending OR increase taxes During inflation (to cool economy)
