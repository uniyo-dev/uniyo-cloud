6.1 Goals of Macroeconomics

Macroeconomics is the branch of economics that studies the ECONOMY AS A WHOLE. It examines aggregate variables like total output (GDP), unemployment, inflation, and economic growth.

Key Macroeconomic Goals:
1. Full Employment — everyone who wants a job has one
2. Price Stability — low and stable inflation
3. Economic Growth — increasing GDP over time
4. Balance of Payments Stability — sustainable trade
5. Income Distribution — fair sharing of wealth

6.2 National Income Accounting

National Income Accounting is the system used to measure the total economic activity of a country. The most important measure is GDP.

Key Definitions:
• GDP (Gross Domestic Product): Total value of all final goods and services produced WITHIN a country's borders in a year
• GNP (Gross National Product): Total value of all final goods and services produced by a country's CITIZENS (wherever they are)

GDP vs GNP

Feature GDP GNP
Basis Geographic (within borders) Nationality (citizens)
Includes All production in Ethiopia Ethiopian citizens worldwide
Excludes Ethiopians abroad Foreigners in Ethiopia
Formula GDP = C + I + G + (X-M) GNP = GDP + Net Factor Income

6.2.1 Approaches to Measure GDP

Three Approaches to Measure GDP:
1. Expenditure Approach: GDP = C + I + G + (X-M)
2. Income Approach: GDP = Wages + Rent + Interest + Profit
3. Production (Value Added) Approach: GDP = Sum of Value Added
Approach What It Measures Formula
Expenditure Total spending on final goods C + I + G + (X-M)
Income Total income earned Wages + Rent + Interest + Profit
Production Value added at each stage Σ (Output - Intermediate inputs)

💡 Real World Example:

Ethiopia's GDP (2023) ≈ $126 billion

Expenditure Approach:
C (Consumption) + I (Investment) + G (Government Spending) + (X-M) (Net Exports)

📌 Key Points to Remember:

  • Macroeconomics = Study of the WHOLE economy
  • GDP = Geographic (within borders) | GNP = Nationality (citizens)
  • Three approaches: Expenditure, Income, Production
  • GDP formula: C + I + G + (X-M)
  • All approaches should give the SAME GDP value
