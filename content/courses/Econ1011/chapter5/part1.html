5.1 The Concept of Market in Physical and Digital Space

A market is any arrangement where buyers and sellers interact to exchange goods and services. Traditionally, markets were PHYSICAL places (like Merkato in Addis Ababa). Today, markets also exist in DIGITAL space (like online shopping platforms).

Key Definition: A market is a mechanism that brings together buyers (demanders) and sellers (suppliers) to determine price and quantity of goods/services.
Market Type Characteristics Examples
Physical Market Face-to-face, tangible location Merkato, local shops, supermarkets
Digital Market Online, virtual platform Amazon, Alibaba, Sheba Online

5.2 Perfectly Competitive Market

Perfect competition is a theoretical market structure where many small firms sell identical products. No single firm can influence the market price.

5.2.1 Assumptions of Perfectly Competitive Market

Key Assumptions:
1. Many buyers and sellers — no single participant affects price
2. Homogeneous (identical) products — no differentiation
3. Perfect information — all participants know everything
4. Free entry and exit — no barriers
5. Price takers — firms accept market price
6. Perfect mobility of resources — resources move freely

💡 Real World Example:

Agricultural markets (like wheat or coffee) approximate perfect competition: many small farmers produce identical crops, no single farmer affects the world price, and new farmers can enter freely.

5.2.2 Short Run Equilibrium of the Firm

In the short run, a perfectly competitive firm faces a HORIZONTAL demand curve (perfectly elastic) — it can sell ANY quantity at the market price.

The firm maximizes profit where MR = MC. In perfect competition, MR = Price (because the firm is a price taker).

5.2.3 Short Run Equilibrium of the Industry

The INDUSTRY (all firms combined) faces a normal DOWNWARD-SLOPING demand curve. The industry price is determined by the intersection of industry supply and demand.

📌 Key Points:

  • Perfect competition: Many firms, identical products, price takers
  • Firm faces HORIZONTAL demand curve (perfectly elastic)
  • Firm maximizes profit where MR = MC = Price
  • Industry has normal downward-sloping demand
  • Free entry and exit → zero economic profit in long run
