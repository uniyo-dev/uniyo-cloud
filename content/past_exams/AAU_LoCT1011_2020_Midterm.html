1. The fundamental economic problem is:

💡 Correct Answer: B) Scarcity

Why B is correct: Scarcity (limited resources vs unlimited wants) is THE fundamental economic problem that all societies face.

Why others are wrong:

  • A) Inflation: This is a RESULT of economic decisions, not the fundamental problem.
  • C) Unemployment: This is a symptom of economic issues, not the root cause.
  • D) Poverty: This is a social condition that can result from scarcity, not the core problem.

📌 Key Concept: Scarcity → Choice → Opportunity Cost
